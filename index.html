Scaffold Design Check

1. Choose the scaffold

Pick the scaffold you are checking. If it is not in the list, ask your Safety Coordinator for the design file.

For the Safety Coordinator

P.E. design details

Type the key points from the P.E. drawing. Leave a box empty if the design does not state it.

2. Take a photo of the scaffold

Stand back so the whole scaffold face is in the photo.

3. Compare with the design

For each point press SAME or WRONG. When you press WRONG, tap the photo where the problem is.

4. Your name and signature
